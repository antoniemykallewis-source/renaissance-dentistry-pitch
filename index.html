What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

No active ads found.

Meta’s selected practice page showed no active U.S. ads. If current, nearby prospects may meet competitors first. October 20, All ads/Active filters; other pages or countries may differ. See advertiser search ↗

02

Smile photos lack context.

Gallery cases have numbers, but no treatment names, goals or timing. A cosmetic patient may like the result yet lack the confidence to request a consultation. See smile gallery ↗

03

The doctor stays in text.

Dr. Krishnamurthy’s detailed bio has no introduction video. Patients cannot hear the approach reviewers praise before making contact. Reviewed bio page only; social videos exist. See doctor page ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Visitors wait, then content moves.

Homepage main content appeared after 4.5 seconds; the phone test also showed layout shifts. Visitors may leave or lose their place. October 20, one slow-4G simulation; not full-page loading or every visitor’s wait. See mobile test ↗

05

Closing times disagree.

Google says weekdays until 5:30pm; the site says 5pm. Patients may call or travel during that half-hour gap. October 20 comparison; confirm correct hours before changing either. See Google hours ↗

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

06

A request has no reply window.

The appointment form requests a time without booking it or stating when staff will reply. A patient ready to schedule may contact another office while waiting. See request form ↗

07

The first screen repeats itself.

Five rotating themes use the same broad practice description. Someone seeking specific care must keep searching rather than seeing a clear reason and route to book. See homepage ↗
