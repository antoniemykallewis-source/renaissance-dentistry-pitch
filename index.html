Why we looked

We looked at the site as a new patient, to see what helps people understand the care and what could make booking clearer.

Finding 1 of 3

Explain each smile result.

What we saw

Gallery photos have case numbers but no treatment names, goals or timing.

Why it matters

Patients see a change without understanding the care.

Our fix

Add short, approved case stories with the goal, treatment and time it took.

See gallery ↗
Finding 2 of 3

Say when patients will hear back.

What we saw

The form requests an appointment; it does not book one or give a reply time.

Why it matters

Patients may not know whether to wait or call.

Our fix

Add a realistic reply window and explain the next step.

See form ↗
Finding 3 of 3

Simplify the first screen.

What we saw

The home page rotates five themes, each with the same general practice description.

Why it matters

Visitors may wait to find the care they want.

Our fix

Lead with one clear welcome. Keep service choices and booking links in place.
